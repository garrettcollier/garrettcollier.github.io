Approach

Public service as institutions, information, and implementation.

Garrett's public-service interests are not limited to holding office or campaign activity. They include how residents receive information, how local government explains decisions, how public systems can become more accessible, and how technology can help institutions work more clearly.

That approach connects his municipal involvement, public-administration education, nonprofit technology work, campaign experience, and civic technology projects. The common question is practical: how can public systems become easier to follow, easier to evaluate, and more responsive to the people they serve?

OpenHotSprings is one expression of that work: a civic-information project built around the idea that public records become more valuable when residents can actually find them, understand them, and follow what happens next.
