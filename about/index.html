About

Garrett Collier

Software developer, public administrator, and public-service practitioner from Hot Springs, Arkansas.

Background

Technology, public administration, and service.

Garrett Collier holds a Master of Public Administration from the University of Southern California and a Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science from Hendrix College. He is from Hot Springs, Arkansas, and his public work has increasingly focused on the relationship between local institutions, civic information, technology, and community problem-solving.

Family, faith, service, and the desire to improve the lives of others through both technology and public work are important parts of Garrett's story. This site is intended to hold that full picture: professional experience, civic work, writing, technical projects, education, research, and the questions that connect them.

Technology

Building practical systems.

Garrett's technical background includes software development, database design, process automation, data systems, and enterprise work. At Acxiom LLC, he has worked as a Solution Developer on secure financial systems projects and earlier projects involving Snowflake, Streamlit, Oracle, Excel automation, and reporting workflows.

At The "I Love U Guys" Foundation, he serves as a Full Stack Developer and works on website development and digital infrastructure supporting school-safety protocols, crisis response, and reunification work.

Public Administration

Studying how institutions work.

Public administration gives Garrett a second lens for understanding systems: government structures, incentives, budgets, accountability, implementation, public trust, and the people affected by policy choices. His writing is meant to translate that kind of analysis into clear explanations for everyday readers.

Public Service

Local government and civic involvement.

Garrett has publicly documented service on the Hot Springs Planning Commission, Board of Zoning and Adjustments, and Arts Advisory Committee. His campaign for Hot Springs City Director belongs in the record of his public-service work, but this personal site treats it as part of a broader biography rather than as a campaign platform.

He also built OpenHotSprings.com to make local meetings, updates, and public information easier for residents to find and follow.

Education & Research

Computer science, public administration, and computational research.

Garrett studied Computer Science at Hendrix College, where his public site notes a focus on software development, database design, process automation, accounting, and economics. He later completed a Master of Public Administration at USC.

During the summer of 2022, he participated in the Advanced Computational Research Experience at Michigan State University and built Python and BASH tools for MRI data analysis workflows.

Values

Substance, clarity, and responsibility.

The common thread across Garrett's technology work, public-service involvement, and writing is a desire to understand complicated systems clearly enough to improve them. That means taking evidence seriously, explaining tradeoffs honestly, and building tools that serve a real public or organizational need.

On this site, those values show up less as slogans than as a working standard: be useful, be clear, take responsibility seriously, and treat public trust as something earned through decisions, not assumed through titles.

For Garrett, public service is broader than a campaign or a single office. It includes the ordinary work of helping institutions explain themselves better, building tools that make public information easier to use, and approaching civic disagreements with enough substance that people can understand the choices in front of them.
