Writing

Academic rigor for everyday people.

Public policy and complicated public questions are often either oversimplified into talking points or buried beneath technical language. I am interested in the space between them.

I use this space to examine questions involving government, technology, economics, institutions, and public policy: what the issue actually is, how the system works, what the evidence tells us, what the tradeoffs are, and where I ultimately land.

The goal is not to tell readers what to think. The goal is to provide enough context, evidence, and explanation for people to understand the issue and reach their own conclusions. Then I explain mine.
