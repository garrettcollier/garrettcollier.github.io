Writing

Academic rigor for everyday people.

Public policy and complicated public questions are often either oversimplified into talking points or buried beneath technical language. I am interested in the space between them.

I examine how systems work, what the evidence supports, and what the tradeoffs mean. The aim is to give readers enough context to reach their own conclusions. Then I explain mine.
