The Intersection

I build systems and study systems.

Software development trains you to understand how complex parts fit together, where information moves, and why a small design choice can shape a whole system. Public administration asks a related set of questions about institutions, budgets, incentives, laws, organizations, and the people those systems are meant to serve.

My work sits in the space between those disciplines: practical technology, public-sector thinking, civic responsibility, and careful explanation. I am interested in tools that make systems clearer, institutions that work better, and writing that gives people enough context to understand complicated public questions for themselves.
